Product Vision 

Product Name

AI Study Assistant

Vision

AI Study Assistant is an intelligent web application designed to help students understand academic topics, find answers to study questions, and improve their learning experience using artificial intelligence.

Problem

Students often struggle to understand complex academic concepts, find reliable explanations, and prepare for exams. Searching across multiple websites can be time-consuming and confusing.

Target Users

The target users are:

University students

High school students

Independent learners

Students preparing for exams

Anyone who wants to learn new topics

Core Value

AI Study Assistant provides a simple and accessible platform where users can:

Ask academic questions and receive AI-generated answers.

Understand difficult topics through clear explanations.

Generate summaries of study materials.

Create practice questions for revision.

Choose different explanation levels based on their knowledge.

Key Features

MVP Features

A text input field for study questions.

An "Ask AI" button.

AI-generated explanations.

A clear area for displaying answers.

Basic error handling and input validation.

Future Features

AI-generated summaries.

Practice quizzes and questions.

Beginner, intermediate, and advanced explanation levels.

User registration and login.

Saved questions and study history.

Personalized learning recommendations.

Learning progress tracking.

Product Goals

Help students learn more efficiently.

Make complex topics easier to understand.

Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

Reduce the time spent searching for explanations.

Encourage independent and continuous learning.
